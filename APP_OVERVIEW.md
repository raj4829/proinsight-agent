# 📊 ProInsight: AI-Powered Business Intelligence Platform

> **Transforming raw data into executive-level decisions with Natural Language and AI.**

ProInsight is a high-performance, agency-grade BI platform designed to bridge the gap between complex data and strategic action. Built for senior consultants and business owners, it automates the entire analytics pipeline—from ingestion to executive reporting.

---

## 🌟 Core Value Proposition

ProInsight eliminates the "Technical Tax" of data analysis. Instead of writing complex SQL or waiting for data teams, stakeholders can interact with their data using **Natural Language**.

- **🕒 Time Savings**: Reduces 20+ hours of manual reporting to under 10 minutes.
- **📈 Revenue Impact**: Identifies hidden growth opportunities through AI trend analysis.
- **🛡️ Accuracy**: Uses DuckDB and OpenAI for precision SQL generation and statistical forecasting.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 💬 Deep Query (NL2SQL)
Ask any business question in plain English. ProInsight's **Agno Agent** translates your intent into highly optimized DuckDB SQL, analyzes the result, and provides natural language insights.
*Example: "Compare return rates for products over $100 against our top sellers."*

### 2. 📈 Interactive Visualizations
Dynamic, high-contrast Plotly charts that bring trends to life.
- **Responsive Charts**: Automatically adjust for mobile or desktop.
- **Deep Drills**: Hover for precise data points and trendline analysis.

### 3. 🔮 AI-Powered Forecasting
Predict the future with **95% statistical confidence**.
- Uses Scikit-learn Linear Regression for trend modeling.
- Generates upper/lower confidence intervals.
- Calculates YoY growth rates and rate of change.

### 4. 📑 Professional Executive Reports
Generate client-ready PDF reports with a single click.
- **Executive Summary**: Automatically synthesized business impact.
- **KPI Grids**: Visual representation of core financial metrics.
- **Insights & Recommendations**: AI-generated strategic advice.

## 💼 Industry Use Cases

### 🛒 E-Commerce: Revenue & Product Optimization
*Focus: Scaling profitability and minimizing losses.*
- **Top Product Analysis**: Identify the 20% of SKUs driving 80% of revenue.
- **Return Rate Mitigation**: Correlation analysis between product categories and return volume.
- **Seasonal Growth**: Forecasting Black Friday demand and Q5 inventory needs.
- **Impact**: **$150K+ revenue opportunity identified** for TechGear electronics.

### 💼 SaaS: Churn & Growth Analytics
*Focus: Predicting retention and accelerating MRR.*
- **MRR Momentum**: Daily and monthly tracking of subscription revenue.
- **Churn Rate Analysis**: Identifying triggers for customer drop-off.
- **Expansion Revenue**: Analyzing upsell opportunities within existing tiers (Free/Pro/Enterprise).
- **Impact**: **68% YoY growth trajectory** achieved for CloudSync.

### 📈 Marketing: ROI & Budget Modeling
*Focus: Maximizing ROAS and minimizing CPA.*
- **Channel Attribution**: Multi-channel comparison (Google, Facebook, LinkedIn, Email).
- **ROAS Maximization**: Identifying high-performing channels with lower CPA.
- **Budget Reallocation**: Modeling the revenue impact of shifting 30% budget between channels.
- **Impact**: **Improved ROAS from 3.2x to 4.1x** for AdVantage Agency.
