## Presentation Tips

### For Live Demos:
1. **Start with Impact**: Lead with "$575K value delivered"
2. **Tell Stories**: Use case study narratives, not feature lists
3. **Show Screenshots**: Visual proof > descriptions
4. **Address Objections**: "Too expensive?" → Show ROI calculator
5. **Clear CTA**: Always end with specific next step

### For Proposals:
1. **Customize**: Reference their specific industry/pain points
2. **Quantify**: Use numbers from similar case studies
3. **Timeline**: "Results in 30 days" builds urgency
4. **Guarantee**: "If you don't see value in 60 days, full refund"

## Objection Handling

**"It's too expensive"**
→ "Let me show you the ROI calculator. For a $1M business, the Professional package pays for itself 9x over through time savings alone, not counting revenue impact."

**"We already have analytics tools"**
→ "Great! Most of my clients had tools too. The difference is I turn your data into specific recommendations. Can your current tool tell you which product to discontinue or which ad channel to double down on?"

**"We need to think about it"**
→ "Absolutely. What specific questions can I answer to help with your decision? Would seeing a sample analysis of your data be helpful?"

**"Can we start with something smaller?"**
→ "Yes! I offer a one-time analysis for $500. You get a full executive report on one specific question. If you love it, we can discuss ongoing work."
